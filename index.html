01 / THE BIG IDEAAbout 4 minutes

A mole is just a giant dozen.

A dozen means 12 things. A mole means 602,214,076,000,000,000,000,000 things. It's simply a counting word.

Same count. Different weight. A dozen feathers and a dozen bowling balls each contain 12 things, but have very different masses. It's the same with a mole of carbon and a mole of water.

YOUR POCKET REFERENCEKeep this handy

The cheat sheet.

Everything important on one page. Come back here whenever a problem feels confusing.

01

Calculate molar mass

Σ (atoms × atomic mass)

H2O = 2(1.008) + 16.00 ≈ 18.02 g/mol

02

Grams → Moles

moles = grams ÷ g/mol

36.04 g of H2O ÷ 18.02 g/mol = 2.00 mol

03

Moles → Grams

grams = moles × g/mol

0.50 mol of CO2 × 44.01 g/mol ≈ 22.0 g

04

Parentheses

Al(OH)3 = Al + 3O + 3H

The outside 3 multiplies everything inside. Also, (NH4)2 has 2 N and 8 H.

05

Scientific notation

1.2 × 103 = 1,200

For tiny amounts: 2.3 × 10−4 = 0.00023. Type 2.3e-4 in the answer box.

06 · BONUS

Moles ↔ Particles

1 mol = 6.022 × 1023 particles

Multiply to get particles. Divide to get moles. For ionic compounds, count formula units.

THE PERIODIC TABLE, SIMPLIFIED
WHEN YOU'RE STUCK
I don't know whether to multiply or divide.+

Write your starting unit and desired unit. Place the unwanted unit on the other side of a fraction so it cancels. If you start with grams, use 1 mol / (molar mass in g). If you start with moles, flip it.

I keep getting the wrong molar mass.+

Write every element separately and multiply by the subscript. In CO2, oxygen contributes 2 × 16.00 = 32.00, not 16.00. A missing subscript means 1.

I don't know if my answer makes sense.+

Estimate first. If a substance has molar mass 18 g/mol, a 9 g sample should be about 0.5 mol. For the same substance, more moles must mean more grams.

Scientific notation is freaking me out.+

“6.022 × 1023” means 6.022 followed by 23 powers of ten. Your calculator may use 6.022E23. Both mean the same thing. In practice, type 6.022e23.
